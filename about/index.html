About Langstaff Door Hardware Co.

A distribution building in Concord will run a single dock-office door through more cycles in a month than a downtown office door sees in three years. That arithmetic is behind most of what we do. Langstaff Door Hardware Co. sizes and services door hardware for buildings where the door is a working part of the operation rather than a piece of trim.

Closers, exit devices, hold-opens, floor springs and power operators — that is the whole trade for us. In Vaughan it means the employment lands first: the warehouses, food plants and freight buildings through Concord and along the 400 and 407, where a closer arm gets torn out of a hollow-metal frame by somebody going through with both hands full, and where the separation between the plant floor and the office has to latch every single time. Then the big-box and showroom entrances near Vaughan Mills and along Highway 7, where a vestibule door faces a hectare of open parking lot and takes wind no interior door ever feels. Then the newer towers at the Vaughan Metropolitan Centre, and the older village main streets in Kleinburg and Woodbridge where the storefront hardware predates most of the people using it.

We reinforce mountings instead of re-screwing into a chewed-out prep, we size units for the traffic they actually see, and we write down what we found so a facilities team has something to act on. The estimate is fixed in scope before anything starts, and if a door only needs adjusting we adjust it and say so.

Why Vaughan property owners trust us

  • Hardware sized for warehouse cycle counts, not catalogue door widths
  • Plant-to-office separations kept latching, with the results in writing
  • Mountings reinforced rather than re-screwed into a chewed-out prep
  • Wind-exposed big-box and vestibule entrances set for the exposure they get
  • Insured, WSIB-covered, one fixed-scope written estimate per job

Insured & WSIB-covered

We carry full liability insurance and WSIB coverage — the certificates most Vaughan warehouse and property managers want before a contractor gets a site pass, and we send them ahead of the visit rather than after it. Ontario issues no provincial licence for door hardware, so we do not pretend to hold one. We guarantee our workmanship in writing instead.
