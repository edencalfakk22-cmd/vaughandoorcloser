Where we work in Vaughan

From Woodbridge, Maple, Concord, Thornhill, Kleinburg, Vaughan Metropolitan Centre, Vellore Village, Patterson, Sonoma Heights, Islington Woods, Beverley Glen and Brownridge, we cover every Vaughan neighbourhood — plus the surrounding York communities.

Warehouse and distribution doors in the Concord and Highway 400 employment lands. Vaughan carries one of the largest industrial bases in the Greater Toronto Area, and most of it sits in the prestige employment lands wrapped around the Highway 400 and 407 corridors — Concord, the Keele and Langstaff blocks, Steeles West, the Highway 27 and Martin Grove strip in west Woodbridge. The doors we get called to in those buildings are not lobby doors. They are dock-office man-doors, warehouse-to-office fire separations and shipping-entrance doors taking hundreds of cycles a shift, plus the occasional pallet jack or skid corner into the arm. Closers on those openings fail from cycle count and impact long before they fail from age, and the arm, shoe bracket and door reinforcement matter more than the badge on the body.

Fire separations in Vaughan's apartment, condo and long-term care stock. Ontario's Fire Code requires the closures in a fire separation to be kept in operating condition, which in plain language means the door swings shut and latches by itself, every time, with nobody helping it. Vaughan Fire and Rescue Service inspects on a schedule and on complaint, and the same findings come up building after building: a stairwell door wedged with an extinguisher during a move-in, a garbage-room door that closes but never latches, a suite door with a closer somebody disconnected years ago because it was heavy. We work those doors building-wide rather than one ticket at a time, and leave a written record of what was found and corrected for the fire safety plan file.
